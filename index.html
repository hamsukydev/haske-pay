Terms of Service

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. About the service

Haske Pay is operated by AYH Hamsuky Enterprises ("we", "us"). It lets you buy electricity tokens, airtime and data using money held in your Haske Pay wallet.

2. Your account

You must provide accurate information and keep your login details and transaction PIN private. You are responsible for activity that happens with your PIN.

3. Wallet and payments

  • You fund your wallet by bank transfer. Funds appear in your wallet once the payment is confirmed.
  • Purchases are paid from your wallet balance. A small service fee may apply and is shown before you confirm.
  • Please check the meter number, phone number and amount carefully. Purchases sent to a wrong number or meter that you entered cannot normally be reversed.

4. Failed and pending purchases

If a purchase fails, the amount is returned to your wallet. If a purchase is pending, we check its status with the service provider and will complete it or refund it once a final result is known. Please do not repeat a pending purchase.

5. Third-party providers

Electricity, airtime and data are delivered through licensed third-party providers. We are not responsible for outages or delays on the networks or distribution companies' side.

6. Acceptable use

You may not use Haske Pay for fraud, money laundering or any unlawful activity. We may suspend accounts that do so.

7. Changes and contact

We may update these terms from time to time. Questions can be sent to yahyaabubakar5@gmail.com.
